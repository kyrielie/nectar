Midnight Blue: a deep navy page (`#1A2735`), light grey body text (`#B3B3B3`) and a
sky-blue link accent (`#529ECC`), set in a flat sans-serif. Links are bold with no
underline until hover, and the AO3 preface sits on a translucent white card.

Ported from the tumblr-blue variant of the `neos` AO3 workskin family by
[ZerafinaCSS/neos](https://github.com/ZerafinaCSS/neos), MIT licensed. This is a
direct color and structure port, not merely inspired by the source. The bundle was
previously named Tumblr Blue; only the name and the descriptive prose changed.

Fonts: Inter and JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL), loaded from Google Fonts by `@import`, so
they fall back to system fonts when offline.
