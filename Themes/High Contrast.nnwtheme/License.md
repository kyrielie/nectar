An accessibility theme: pure white page with black text in light mode, pure black page with white text in dark mode, both well past WCAG AAA (7:1) for body text and links. Links are always underlined and thick, with a distinct visited color and a strong focus outline. Text is set larger than System, at a 1.7 line height, left-aligned and unhyphenated, with wider paragraph spacing. It honors Dynamic Type on iOS. AO3 prefaces get a solid border and the series footer is set at full size.

Original work for Nectar, MIT licensed like the repository.

Fonts: Atkinson Hyperlegible by the Braille Institute of America, SIL Open Font License 1.1, copyright 2020 Braille Institute of America, Inc. The latin subset in regular, bold, italic and bold italic is embedded as base64 woff2 (taken from `@fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible` 5.3.0, OFL-1.1), so the theme works offline and needs no network request.
