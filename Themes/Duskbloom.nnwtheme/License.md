Dark mode ported from the Charcoal Rose variant of the `neos` AO3 workskin

Light mode palette *inspired by* intothisshadow's Moonlit Wisteria AO3 skin
([so-obsessed.com](https://so-obsessed.com))

Combined into one theme that follows the system light/dark setting, with a
shared shape language, link/dateline/title treatment, and typography
applied consistently across both palettes.
