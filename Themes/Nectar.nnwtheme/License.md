Nectar is the house theme: the look of the Nectar landing page and theme gallery, set as a reader theme. Warm paper, ink, madder red and gold, with a William Morris style leaf frieze, gold double rules, a fleuron divider and a madder drop cap. Light is paper and ink, dark is plum and cream.
