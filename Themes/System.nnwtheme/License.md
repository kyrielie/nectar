The default Nectar look: the system font at your Dynamic Type size, black text on
white in light mode and white on black in dark mode, and the system blue for
links. A small header shows the feed name and byline above the title. AO3 work
prefaces and series footers use neutral greys that follow the color scheme.

Derived from the default article stylesheet and template of
[NetNewsWire](https://github.com/Ranchero-Software/NetNewsWire) (MIT licensed,
Brent Simmons and contributors), with Nectar's AO3 additions. This bundle is
also the app's built-in fallback theme: the two copies of `stylesheet.css` and
`template.html` are kept byte-identical.

No third-party fonts or assets; the system font is used.
