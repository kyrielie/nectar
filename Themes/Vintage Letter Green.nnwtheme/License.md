Inspired by the AO3 workskin "Vintage Letter Green" by
[Wolfbatcat/Vintage-Letter-Green-AO3-Skin](https://github.com/Wolfbatcat/Vintage-Letter-Green-AO3-Skin).


That skin is a whole-site overlay built around a fixed background
photograph and cream (`#fdfaec`) card panels floating on it; there's no
article-reading-page equivalent of a floating photo backdrop here, so what's
ported is its actual palette and character — warm cream panels, deep green
ink and accent.
