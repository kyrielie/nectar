# Catalogue Bevel

A library-catalogue record: a glossy accent bar with a 2 by 2 cluster of square type icons carries the title, chapter headings are beveled buttons, tags are dotted-underlined, and the story sits in a bordered record. Deep red on white; blue on charcoal at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Archive Of Your Own* by *wovenstarlight, an homage to a fan-fiction library site*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Libre Baskerville, Impallari Type, SIL OFL 1.1; Noto Sans, Google, SIL OFL 1.1).
