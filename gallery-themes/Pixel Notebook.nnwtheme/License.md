# Pixel Notebook

A two-color notebook: everything is ink on paper with chunky borders, hover and tap swap ink and paper, notes get triple double-lines, and the story sits on ruled paper with a spiral binding punched down the left edge. Sepia ink on cocoa by night; brown ink on parchment by day.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Haunted* by *wovenstarlight, design by Bwob*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Pixelify Sans, Stefie Justprince, SIL OFL 1.1; Courier Prime, Alan Dague-Greene, SIL OFL 1.1).
