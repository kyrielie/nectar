# Late Night Lounge

A chat-room page: the title sits in a channel bar with a round hash badge that squares off on touch, the chapters read as one message column, and chapter breaks are the punched date dividers of a chat log. Teal accent; soft grey panels in light mode, charcoal in dark.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Discord* by *wovenstarlight*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source).
