# Vermilion Cap

A stage with an orange pill cap: the title plate is a full-bleed vermilion pill with a small ringed circle, three stepped bars (30, 20, 10 pixels wide) step down the story's right edge, and quotes open with a giant curly mark. Vermilion on light grey; vermilion on near-black at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *daemonia* by *egg.design*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source).
