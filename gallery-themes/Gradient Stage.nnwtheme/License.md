# Gradient Stage

Floating cards on a calm stage: every panel has a 3px two-stop gradient border and a deep offset shadow, and the title carries a cascade of three shrinking portrait discs. Rosewood and slate on neutral grey; the same gradient on charcoal at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *cat in a box* by *octomoosey*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Yanone Kaffeesatz, Yanone, SIL OFL 1.1; Roboto, Christian Robertson, Apache 2.0).
