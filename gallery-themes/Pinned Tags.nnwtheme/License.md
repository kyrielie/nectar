# Pinned Tags

A painted banner runs behind the page and the title hangs across its seam on a paper tag with a punched hole; the story sits on a sheet that straddles the same seam. Bold and italic text carry their own colors. Mossy green by day, warm brown by night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Paint Job* by *wovenstarlight*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Abel, Matthew Desmond, SIL OFL 1.1).
