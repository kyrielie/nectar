# Graph Desk

A graph-paper desk: the page is a blue grid, the title is a slightly tilted notepaper card held by a strip of translucent tape, and chapter headings are colored index tabs. Teal and sky on pale grid paper; deep teal at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Gummy Shark* by *egg.design*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Poppins, Indian Type Foundry, SIL OFL 1.1; Caveat, Impallari Type, SIL OFL 1.1).
