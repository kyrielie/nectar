# Hung Dates

A picture-framed page: a solid mat frame is drawn around the screen edge, the story is a narrow column with a hairline down its left side, chapter headings hang out into the margin in small lowercase, and notes are dark sidebar boxes with orange accents. Charcoal and orange on grey; the inverse at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *baby* by *octomoosey*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Work Sans, Wei Huang, SIL OFL 1.1).
