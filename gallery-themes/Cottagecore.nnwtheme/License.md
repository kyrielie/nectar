Cottagecore is a mockup-derived theme: background #fbf6ea, text #4a3b32, accent None, headings in Cormorant Garamond over body text in EB Garamond, with a dark-mode palette.

Source: designed as a CSS mockup for Nectar (Batch 7) and converted to this bundle by script. License: not yet decided; set one before publishing.

Fonts: loaded from Google Fonts by `@import` (Caveat, Cormorant Garamond, EB Garamond). Per-family licenses were not checked.
