# Tape Crossing

Washi tape laid across the page like a desk: the title runs on one wide horizontal strip and the byline on a second, shorter one, a tape runs down the story's right edge, and a marbled L-shaped corner piece caps the top. Plum and lilac in light mode, forest and lime at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Washi 2* by *wovenstarlight*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Josefin Sans, Santiago Orozco, SIL OFL 1.1; Lora, Cyreal, SIL OFL 1.1).
