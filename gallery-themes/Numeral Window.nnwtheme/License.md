# Numeral Window

A monochrome window: a solid title strip, a one-pixel frame, photos in greyscale until touched (a slow 0.9 second fade), and chapter headings numbered in roman numerals by a CSS counter. Black and white with one crimson accent; inverted at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *fiddle of gold* by *egg.design*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Lato, Lukasz Dziedzic, SIL OFL 1.1).
