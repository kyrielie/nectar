# Fimfiction

A light and dark theme that follows the look of Fimfiction's reader. Content sits in a bordered, rounded box on a warm tan page (light) or a hatched slate page (dark), with a dark site bar across the top. Titles are Open Sans Condensed-style bold, the chapter heading carries a hairline rule, work info reads as colored tag pills (rating, warning, genre, character, series), notes are Author's Note boxes, and scene breaks use the triple fleuron rule. Follows the system appearance: light uses the site's light palette, dark uses its dark palette.

## Where it came from

Inspired by the default light and dark skins of [Fimfiction](https://www.fimfiction.net). Color and type values were read from the site's published stylesheets and reimplemented here for Nectar's markup; no site code, images or icon fonts are copied. Fimfiction is not affiliated with Nectar, and the source's license was not checked, so this bundle makes no claim to a grant from it. Rating mapping is approximate: AO3's General, Teen and Mature map to Fimfiction's Everyone, Teen and Mature; Explicit uses the Mature color; Not Rated stays neutral.

## Third-party assets

Set in Open Sans (Steve Matteson, SIL OFL) via Google Fonts. The headings use the family's condensed width, the closest Google Fonts match to Fimfiction's Open Sans Condensed.

Fonts are not bundled; they are loaded from Google Fonts at read time and remain under their own license.
