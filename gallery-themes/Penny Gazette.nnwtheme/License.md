# Penny Gazette

A typeset broadsheet drawn with rules and type alone: a thick-thin masthead, a boxed byline between thin rules, a perforated postage stamp built from one radial gradient, a drop cap, and photos that warm from sepia on touch. Kraft paper and ink red; lamplit charcoal in dark.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Newsprint* by *wovenstarlight*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (IM FELL English SC, Igino Marini, SIL OFL 1.1; Zen Old Mincho, Yoshimichi Ohira, SIL OFL 1.1).
