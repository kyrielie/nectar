# Tracked Mist

Soft translucent tiles drifting over a misty gradient sky: the title is set in light, very widely tracked capitals that open up further on touch. Dusk blue and violet at night; pearl and blue by day.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *myst* by *egg.design*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Raleway, Matt McInerney, Pablo Impallari and Rodrigo Fuenzalida, SIL OFL 1.1).
