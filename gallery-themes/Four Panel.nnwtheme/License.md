# Four Panel

A webcomic page: a bordered title frame, the story in a framed panel, and chapter breaks as gutters between panels. Every frame shares one teal border; headings are marigold on deep teal in dark mode and ochre on paper in light.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Sans Comic* by *wovenstarlight, concept by kernelbastard*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source).
