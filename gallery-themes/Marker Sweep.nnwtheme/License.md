# Marker Sweep

Two color pairs and a highlighter pen: links carry a marker stripe that thickens on touch, quotes are threads with a square terminal dot, and the title sits in a wash-tinted hero panel. Wine and lavender at night; navy and crimson by day.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Horizon* by *wovenstarlight*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (DM Sans, Colophon Foundry and Indian Type Foundry, SIL OFL 1.1).
