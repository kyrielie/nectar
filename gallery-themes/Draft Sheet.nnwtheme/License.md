# Draft Sheet

A word-processor page: the story is a white paper sheet with a hairline outline and a soft lift, headings carry an outline dash, chapter breaks are dashed page breaks, and every tint is a low-alpha mix of one text color and one accent. Blue accent; paper on grey in light, charcoal in dark.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Document* by *wovenstarlight, an homage to a web word processor*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Roboto, Christian Robertson, Apache 2.0).
