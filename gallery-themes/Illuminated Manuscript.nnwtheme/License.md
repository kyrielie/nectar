Illuminated Manuscript is a mockup-derived theme: background #efe0bd, text #2b1c10, accent #9c1f1f, headings in UnifrakturCook over body text in EB Garamond, with a dark-mode palette.

Source: designed as a CSS mockup for Nectar (Batch 4) and converted to this bundle by script. License: not yet decided; set one before publishing.

Fonts: loaded from Google Fonts by `@import` (EB Garamond, UnifrakturCook). Per-family licenses were not checked.
