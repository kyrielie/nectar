# Tape Strips

A scrapbook journal where headings, tags and quotes are strips of patterned tape: three tape colors cycle down the page, the title tape ends in a pointed tip, and a faint woven print (drawn with gradients, no images) lies over each strip. Green, pink, cream and sky tape on a pale page; plum and amber at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Washi 1* by *wovenstarlight*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets
