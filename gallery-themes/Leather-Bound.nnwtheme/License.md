Leather-Bound is a mockup-derived theme: background #f1e5c8, text #2b2018, accent None, headings in Cormorant SC over body text in EB Garamond, with a dark-mode palette.

Source: designed as a CSS mockup for Nectar (Batch 8) and converted to this bundle by script. License: not yet decided; set one before publishing.

Fonts: loaded from Google Fonts by `@import` (Cormorant SC, EB Garamond). Per-family licenses were not checked.
