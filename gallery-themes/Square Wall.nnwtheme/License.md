# Square Wall

A photo-profile page: the title is a profile card with a round two-tone avatar, pictures sit as rounded squares, chapter headings are outlined buttons that sweep to a solid fill on touch, and chapter breaks are three small squares. Pink and blue accents; ghost white or charcoal.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Social Media* by *palemomos (Themes by Pale)*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). The source's published terms forbid copying its code or recreating it elsewhere, so this is a different design: it shares no markup, names, code or art, and only uses general techniques.

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Nunito Sans, Vernon Adams and Jacques Le Bailly, SIL OFL 1.1).
