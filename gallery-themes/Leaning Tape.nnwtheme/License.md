# Leaning Tape

Tape strips lean against a calm, straight story column: the title tape tilts up and to the left, chapter tabs lean the other way and slide on touch, and the body stays level. Rust, orange and magenta on wine at night; teal and sky tape on sea-glass by day.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Washi 3* by *wovenstarlight*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source).
