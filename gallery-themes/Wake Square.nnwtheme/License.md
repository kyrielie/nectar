# Wake Square

A 600px square that wakes up: the black title container sits at half brightness with a pointer and comes to full brightness on hover (always full on touch screens), the story is a pale port, and chapter headings are charcoal cards with a single orange accent. Black and grey; the same inverted at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *trash* by *octomoosey*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Barlow, Jeremy Tribby, SIL OFL 1.1).
