# Lilac Card

A soft app card built from three colors: the page is a 20 percent accent tint, the story sits on a bordered card with a corner notch, and an overlapping round badge crosses the header edge. Lilac by day, magenta-on-black at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Unite* by *wovenstarlight, an homage to the Tumblr blog view*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Nunito, Vernon Adams and contributors, SIL OFL 1.1).
