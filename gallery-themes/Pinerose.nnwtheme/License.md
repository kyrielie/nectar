Adaptive merge of the Rosé Pine Dawn (light) and Rosé Pine Moon (dark)
variants of the `ao3-rose-pine` workskin by BlackBatCat,
[Wolfbatcat/ao3-rose-pine](https://github.com/Wolfbatcat/ao3-rose-pine),
cited MIT in this theme's `stylesheet.css` comments.

**License status is unconfirmed** — carried forward from both source
themes: no `LICENSE` file could be found in the source repository, and the
source skin itself credits
[ZerafinaCSS/neos](https://github.com/ZerafinaCSS/neos) (MIT) as its own
base. See `Rosé Pine.nnwtheme/License.md` for the full note.

Structural and color differences between Dawn and Moon (link underline
style, hover treatment, article title style, footnote-popover elevation,
chapter-fetch-notice accent) are now expressed as a single stylesheet
switching on `prefers-color-scheme` rather than as two separate ported
themes; see `docs/pinerose-merge-scope.md` for the merge this was built
from. Dawn and Moon remain available as standalone, pinned-appearance
installs.
