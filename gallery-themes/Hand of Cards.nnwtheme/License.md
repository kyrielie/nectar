# Hand of Cards

A game profile hub: the title is a collectible card with a gold badge, the story sits on a rounded panel with card-tab chapter headings, and the page ends in a frosted-glass pager strip. Light-blue accent on navy in dark; the same on pale sky in light.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Rhythmic* by *wovenstarlight, an homage to a rhythm-game profile screen*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Exo 2, Natanael Gama, SIL OFL 1.1).
