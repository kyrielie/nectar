# Dusk Cards

Question-and-answer cards at twilight: the title is a lavender question card with a star badge notched out of its corner, chapter headings are peach-to-gold answer cards, and quotes are salmon panels with a soft border. The page stays plain so the cards carry the color. Indigo at night; pale lavender by day.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Starlight FAQ* by *wovenstarlight*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Lato, Lukasz Dziedzic, SIL OFL 1.1).
