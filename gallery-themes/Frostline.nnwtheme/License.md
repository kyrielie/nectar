# Frostline

A winter page: wide-tracked small capitals, a single accent rule under the title, and a header photo-glow that melts into the page color. Orange on deep navy at night; steel blue on pale ice by day.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Subzero* by *wovenstarlight*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Jost, Owen Earl, SIL OFL 1.1).
