# Ribbon Spread

A bound book: the story page has a rounded outer edge and a thick spine rule, the title is set in blackletter on a cover-colored plate with an asymmetric leaf corner, and chapter headings are ribbon markers with a swallowtail end. Terracotta and parchment by day; terracotta on near-black at night.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *gothica* by *egg.design*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets
