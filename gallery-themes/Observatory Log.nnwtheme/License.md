# Observatory Log

A star-chart notebook drawn with hairlines: a crosshair marker on the title frame, L-shaped viewfinder brackets around the story, and a small planet with a tilted ring as the chapter divider. One amber accent; every tint derives from it.

Original design for Nectar. Inspired by *Jupiter* by *wovenstarlight*. It was read for technique and palette only; the layout, the copy, the names and every drawn shape are new and written from scratch as plain CSS (no code, images, icons or fonts from the source). 

## Third-party assets

Fonts are not bundled; they load from Google Fonts at read time and keep their own licenses (Space Grotesk, Florian Karsten, SIL OFL 1.1; Source Serif 4, Adobe, SIL OFL 1.1).
